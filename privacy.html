Privacy Policy — MC Parrot

Last updated: 5 September 2026
Short version: MC Parrot does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. Everything you record is processed on your own device and never leaves it. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers of ours involved.

Who is responsible

MC Parrot is developed and published by Marcello Massardo, an individual developer. This policy applies to the MC Parrot Android application and to the MC Parrot web application.

What data we collect

None. We do not collect, request or receive any personal information: no name, no email address, no account, no contacts, no location, no device identifiers, no advertising ID, no usage analytics, no crash reporting.

How your audio is handled

  • The app records audio from your microphone only while you actively press record.
  • The recording is analysed and turned into music entirely on your device, by the app's own code. Nothing is uploaded, streamed or sent anywhere.
  • Recordings and generated tracks are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • A file leaves your device only if you choose to export or share it, using your device's own sharing features, to a destination you pick.

Permissions we ask for, and why

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) — required: it is the raw material the app turns into music. Used only during an active recording started by you.
  • Modify audio settings — used to configure the audio input for recording and playback quality.
  • Internet — the app itself works fully offline. This permission exists because the app's interface runs in a local web view, and to open external links (such as the donation page) in your browser if you tap them. No audio or personal data is ever sent over the network.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising networks, no analytics SDKs, no social network SDKs and no tracking libraries of any kind. We do not share data with anyone, because we do not have any.

If you choose to make a donation, the payment happens on PayPal's own website, under PayPal's privacy policy. We never see or handle your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Since no personal data is collected or stored by us, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on your behalf. The data created by the app lives on your device and is under your control: you can delete it at any time from within the app, or by uninstalling the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: the project page on GitHub.
